HOW TO BUILD IT (this method worked well for me before)
- Write the whole film as one HTML/CSS/JS page with a single function seek(t)
  that sets every element's state for time t. No CSS transitions or timers:
  everything must be a pure function of t so frames are repeatable.
- Render it frame by frame with headless Chromium (Playwright): call seek(t),
  screenshot, pipe the frames into ffmpeg. Use my machine's cores in parallel.
- Build each book as a real 3D object in CSS (preserve-3d): front, back, spine,
  page edges, a cover that swings open and pages that flip. Use my photos as
  the textures, perspective-corrected and with the lighting evened out.
- IMPORTANT for sharpness: lay out the 3D book faces at 3x their on-screen size
  and scale the book down with the transform, and render at device scale
  factor 2. Otherwise the browser draws the photos small and enlarges them,
  and they come out blurry.
- Remove fingers and thumbs from photos. Do not invent page content: if a page
  photo is missing part of the page, leave it blank and tell me.
- When showing an inside page, zoom in until the highlighted passage nearly
  fills the width of the screen, then animate a highlight over the exact words.

SOUND
